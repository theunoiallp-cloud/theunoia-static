Meet the Squad

The makers behind
beautiful thinking.

India is full of talented students who don’t need to wait until graduation to prove what they can do. Across campuses, students are already designing, coding, writing, building, and creating. Yet meaningful opportunities often remain out of reach, while businesses struggle to find capable talent; sometimes just a hostel room away.

That disconnect doesn’t have to continue. Eunoia means beautiful thinking, and with TH, we turned that idea into a purpose: to look beyond resumes and degrees, uncover overlooked talent, and bring their abilities into the real world.

We’re a team of student makers building Hunhar and Hivh; a student-first freelance ecosystem connecting emerging talent with real opportunities. Hustlers get to earn through meaningful projects, while businesses find capable people to get the work done. These are the people building it.
